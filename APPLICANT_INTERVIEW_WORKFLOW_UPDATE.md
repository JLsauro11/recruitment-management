# Applicant and Interview Workflow Update

- Applicants is now the master list and shows the current hiring stage.
- Applicants shows interview count and the latest interview summary.
- A calendar action in Applicants opens the Interview page with the applicant preselected.
- Interviews is labeled as schedule/history and distinguishes Applicant Stage from Interview Status.
- Scheduling or completing an interview automatically synchronizes the applicant stage.
- Deleting or moving an interview recalculates the applicant stage to prevent stale or mismatched data.
- Duplicate active schedules for the same applicant and interview type are blocked.
- Fixed duplicate JavaScript variable declaration in the Interview form.
