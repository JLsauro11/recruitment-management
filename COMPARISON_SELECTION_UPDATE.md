# Candidate Comparison Selection Update

- Candidate Comparison no longer renders all applicant evidence immediately.
- HR/Admin must select 2 to 4 applicants and click **Compare Selected**.
- Only selected candidates are displayed in the side-by-side table.
- Stronger verified values are highlighted green; weaker verified values are highlighted red; ties/N/A remain neutral.
- Comparison rows now expose specific evidence, including verified total experience duration, role-relevant experience, job records, role-requirement matches, questionnaire coverage, degree/certification context, problem-solving signals, and availability/notice period.
- Experience duration is never invented. Older applications without an end date/current-employment flag remain "not verifiable" until the record is completed.
- A specific comparison summary is generated from the selected candidates instead of automatically comparing rank #1 and #2.
